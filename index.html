[ 01 ] · about me

From Coursework to Real Code

I'm a Data Science & AI graduate student from Kolkata, currently pursuing my M.Sc at RKMVERI after a B.Sc (Honours) in Mathematics. That math background is what I lean on most — it's why I care as much about why a model works as about getting it to run.

I like taking what I learn in class — machine learning theory, statistical modeling, algorithms — and immediately testing it by building something with it. That habit has produced a growing set of independent projects and research work, from reinforcement learning environments and deep learning for sports analytics to a structured literature review on ML applications in mental health. I'm always looking for the next problem worth digging into.
